Rig focus · ChArUco cube

Put the cube 2–3 m in front of the lens. Turn the ring until the score peaks, overshoot, come back to PEAK.
Score = Laplacian variance of the cube region, normalised for square size and contrast (relative — no absolute "sharp"). Best is the highest score seen for that camera since start / reset; PEAK ≥ 97 % of best, NEAR ≥ 85 %, LOW below. Reset the best after you move the cube or re-aim the camera. NO CUBE = no marker detected (move the cube into view, or the lens is very far out of focus).
